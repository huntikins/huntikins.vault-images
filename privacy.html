Privacy

huntikins.vault is a private tool used by one eBay seller to keep track of their own inventory and sales. It is not offered to anyone else.

When it reads data from eBay, it keeps only the seller's own sale records: order numbers, item numbers, titles, prices, fees, shipping label costs and dates. It does not store buyer names, usernames, addresses, email addresses or phone numbers, and it does not share any data with third parties.

This site hosts product photos of the seller's own cards and nothing else.
